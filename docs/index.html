About RRARSC

A thriving artistic roller skating community since the 1980s

Reigate and Redhill Artistic Roller Skating Club was founded by Barry Bridger in the 1980s. What started as a handful of competitive skaters meeting once a week has grown into a large and thriving club with close to 100 members.

As a club we no longer compete, preferring to focus on skating for enjoyment rather than the pressure of training several days a week. But our history is proud – over 40 years, our skaters have won over 100 medals at national events across free skating, dance, figures, pairs and entertainment. We've even had a skater represent Great Britain at the World Artistic Inline Skating Championships!

Our coaches and skaters pride themselves on offering the chance to skate to absolutely everybody. Members come from all walks of life to enjoy improving their skating together with friends and family.

For those who simply want to enjoy skating, we organise sessions where you can skate around and meet new people. For those attracted to the challenge of improving, our coaches will teach you through our Basic Skills Grades and beyond.

Skaters who pass our Basic Skills Grades can continue to learn more technical elements like spins and jumps, then combine all their skills to perform in our shows.

It doesn't matter how good you are – just come along and enjoy yourselves! And yes, adults can skate too!

Basic Skills Grades

Our structured progression system helps skaters build confidence and master new skills

Becoming a confident skater takes time and practice. The club uses a basic skills grading system – a structured progression from skills that build confidence to skills that really test balance, control and enthusiasm.

Grades 1-3

Building Foundations

Learn to stand, fall safely, and get moving. Progress through basic stroking, T-stops, and snake patterns. Build confidence with balance exercises and backward skating basics.

Grades 4-6

Developing Skills

Master progressive runs, edges, and arabesques. Learn your first spins and turns including outside 3-turns, Mohawks, and cross rolls. Develop fluid movement and control.

Grades 7-9

Advanced Techniques

Perfect back spins, spreads, and jumps. Learn step sequences and field movements. Grade 9 culminates in a 1-minute programme to music showcasing all your skills.

Once you've passed the 9 basic grades, there are 9 more for advanced skaters to tackle.

Skaters who reach grade 9 can become junior coaches, learning to teach others and attending higher grade sessions. The club also participates in the Duke of Edinburgh award scheme.

Performances

Your chance to shine

Although we no longer compete as a club, we still provide opportunities to perform.

Once you've reached the higher grades, you can perform in our summer exhibition – a chance to showcase your skills in front of an audience.

We also put on a Christmas show with group performances, festive fun, and just a hint of panto!
