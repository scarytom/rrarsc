When we Skate

For all of our sessions open to the general public we have listed the locations and times of when they take place:


Last Saturday of the Month Disco

Either at The Hawthorns School, Bletchingley, (or occasionally at Royal Alexandra and Albert School in Gatton Park) - General Skate around with games, fun for all

The Last Saturday of every Month. 6:00pm - 8:00pm

If you need to hire skates, best to let Barry know before the day.


Fun Skate

Saturdays at the Hawthorns School Sports Centre RH1 4QJ General Skate

Every Saturday 12.45pm to 1.45pm General skate for all ages.

Lessons

The Hawthorns School Sports Centre - Grades 1 to 9

Every Saturday after the Fun Skate. 2:00pm - 3:00pm

Lessons follow Federation grades for all ages.


The club also offers the opportunity for Skating Parties. To find out more contact Barry Bridger on O7944 281862
