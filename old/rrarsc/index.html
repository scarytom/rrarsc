Redhill and Reigate Artistic Roller Skating Club

All of the Skaters and Coaches at Redhill and Reigate Artistic Roller Skating Club (RRARSC) would like to Welcome you to their website. Please feel free to have a look around the website and learn more about the sport of Artistic Roller Skating.

The website is forever changing and updating with News about Shows, Charity Events and Training Sessions which members of the club have organised. The club was originally founded to promote and teach Artistic Roller Skating, and has long since expanded into more modern and "popular" forms of skating. We also run a family skate session at The Hawthorns School sports centre, at which skaters of all ages are welcome. The family skate is followed by lessons for those who want to become confident on skates, or just get fitter.

If you are interested in joining, we offer both Coaching Sessions and Discos for all ages, no matter how experienced you are. Also, we have around 200 pairs of Quad skates and Blades available for hire meaning that you don't even need to own a pair of skates to come along!
