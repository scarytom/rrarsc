LAST SATURDAY OF THE MONTH ROLLER DISCO

Last Saturday of the Month Roller Discos are at The Hawthorns, from 6 til 8. We often have themed nights (Halloween, Holidays) so check with Barry (on O7944 281862) and you can reserve skates at the same time. Reservations must be done before by texting your email address to O7944 281862. We will then send you our booking and payment info. Once you have this you must complete all bookings and payments by 10pm on the Wednesday prior

 

The Hawthorns School Sports Hall, Pendell Court, Bletchingley, RH1 4QJ
